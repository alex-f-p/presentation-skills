# Brief output example

An example `Brief` section. Adapt the headings and leave out what does not apply.

```markdown
## Brief

### Audience and outcome
Working professionals with mixed familiarity with AI drafting tools.
Participants should identify a suitable drafting task and explain what
still needs human review.

### Success
Participants can name a checking step and distinguish a drafting-time
reduction from a reduction in the complete workflow.

### Use and constraints
A 20-minute live lunch-and-learn, including five minutes for discussion,
with a readable take-away reference. Define unfamiliar terms and describe
the comparison aloud. Room and access needs are not yet known.

### Scope and evidence
One workflow, one comparison and a short application exercise.
Exclude a tool tour and general productivity promises. The timing figures
are invented, not measured: drafting falls from 30 to 12 minutes; review
stays at 60.

### Assumptions and preferences
Assumption: participants recognise drafting and approval as separate steps.
Preference: practical language, a warm voice and minimal introductory framing.

### Continuation
Confirm viewing conditions before slide production. This request ends
with the brief; the next useful task is a content outline.
```
