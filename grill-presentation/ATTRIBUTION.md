The interview method is adapted from Matt Pocock’s `grill-me` and `grilling` skills in [mattpocock/skills](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills), reviewed at commit `5863806dd4e15c63174bee4e7515a4256792ecfa`:

- [grilling](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/5863806dd4e15c63174bee4e7515a4256792ecfa/skills/productivity/grilling/SKILL.md)
- [grill-me documentation](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/5863806dd4e15c63174bee4e7515a4256792ecfa/docs/productivity/grill-me.md)

This adaptation retains dependent questioning, investigation of available facts and iterative refinement. It adds presentation-specific branches, a concise brief, readiness for the next task and optional routing to related presentation skills. It replaces exhaustive branching and blanket confirmation with scope-aware completion.
