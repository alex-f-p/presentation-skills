# Using related presentation skills

Four companion skills may be installed: `brief-presentation`, `develop-presentation`, `design-presentation-slides` and `review-and-rehearse-presentation`. Call one through the host’s skill mechanism when a small, specific result from it would settle a current question or do the next task the user asked for. The interview works without them.

| Skill | Call it when | Ask for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `brief-presentation` | The audience, outcome or delivery conditions have changed and need consolidating. | An updated brief and the gaps that matter, built on the decisions already made. Skip it if the working brief is adequate. |
| `develop-presentation` | A message, chain of evidence, prerequisite or sequence needs testing. | A short argument, an alternative opening or an outline. Ask for a detailed storyboard only if the task needs one. |
| `design-presentation-slides` | A question about representation or readability needs something to look at. | Design advice, or one representative rendered scene if production is in scope. |
| `review-and-rehearse-presentation` | An existing outline, deck, source claim or rehearsal may hide a problem that matters. | Findings on that question only, tied to the evidence available, with untested conditions named. |

For example, sketch two openings with Develop when they serve different audience needs. Render one scene with Design when a diagram’s whole-to-detail mapping is in doubt and production is in scope. Use Review to check whether a slide overstates its source before asking the user to settle the presentation’s promise.

## Handing over

Pass the settled decisions, the sources or files involved, the constraints, the exact open question and any qualification that must survive. You own the interview: tell the companion not to repeat intake or start an interview of its own. When the result comes back, update the brief and reopen only the questions it affects.

## Scope

Stay within what the user asked for. Short wording and outline alternatives in the conversation are part of an interview. Creating or editing a deck is not, unless the user asked for it. If a render or observation is out of scope or unavailable, record the check that is needed. Never ask the user to guess its result, and never report it as done.

## If a companion is missing

Carry on with this skill and the tools available: assemble the brief, sketch a short argument, compare a claim with its source or name the representation that needs testing. Report what could not be produced or inspected. Do not install skills or depend on files from other skill folders.

## Finishing

Pass the brief straight to the next requested task. Settled decisions stay settled. The companions can be used in any order, and none is required.
