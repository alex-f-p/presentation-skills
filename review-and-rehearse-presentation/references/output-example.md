# Review output example

An example `Review` section for a text-only review of one claim. Adapt it to the scope and the evidence available.

```markdown
## Review

### Scope and evidence

Checked: draft 1, slide 4 title and the supplied timing figures only.
Source: [invented teaching figures](sources/timing-example.md), not measurements.
Not checked: rendered slides, delivery and audience understanding.

### Finding

Location: slide 4 title, “60% less total time”.
Severity: high. The claim misleads.

Evidence: drafting falls from 30 to 12 minutes; review stays at 60.
The 60% reduction applies to drafting. Total time falls from 90 to
72 minutes, a 20% reduction.

Audience consequence: the title overstates the whole-workflow gain
and could support an unjustified rollout decision.

Proposed repair: retitle to “Total time falls from 90 to 72 minutes”.
Keep the “illustrative figures” label and the unchanged review stage visible.

### Continuation

No edits were requested or made, so nothing was rechecked. Next: correct
the title, then check its wording, label and affected exports against
the source.
```

For an observed rehearsal, add its revision, conditions, time allowed, measured duration, timestamped findings and any recheck. Report pacing as an estimate unless a run-through was observed.
