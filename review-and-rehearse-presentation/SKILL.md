# Review and rehearse a presentation

Judge whether the presentation works for its audience and intended use, and fix what the request covers.

## Read first

- [Editorial rules](references/editorial-rules.md) and [integrated review](references/integrated-review.md) for any review of a deck, slides or content.
- [Rehearsal and observation](references/rehearsal-and-observation.md) for delivery, recordings or pacing.

## Steps

1. Start from the supplied deck, outline, notes or recording. Reconstruct missing context only where a finding depends on it.
2. Check against the evidence you have. Compare claims with their sources. Judge visuals from rendered output, not from source files. Read any reading version with no narration. If sources or renders are missing, review what you can and name what you could not check.
3. Check the deck against the editorial rules and report each breach as a finding.
4. Rank the findings. False evidence, misleading omissions and unreadable essential content come first.
5. For each finding, give its location, the evidence, the consequence for the audience, its severity and the smallest repair that fixes it.
6. Make the repairs the request covers, then recheck every output they affect.
7. Send each remaining problem to where it starts. Weak claims, missing prerequisites and overruns are content problems. A chart or diagram that misleads is a design problem. A changed audience or outcome means the brief needs revising.

## Rules

- Stay within the scope of the request, for review and for repair.
- Keep observed defects, open questions and untested conditions apart.
- Keep pacing estimates, observed delivery and audience responses apart.
- Never report a successful rehearsal from reading a script, or audience understanding from your own reading.
- Fix an overrun by cutting scope. Faster speech and smaller type are not fixes.

## Output

Return the ranked findings, the changes made, the revision and formats checked, and what remains unchecked. For each repair made, give the recheck result or say why it could not be rechecked. Chat-only requests stay in chat, and a small review needs no record.

For a saved project, write to its working document, or to `presentation.md` beside the presentation files. Read the existing sections first and update `Review`, naming the revision each finding concerns. Refer to earlier checks; do not copy them. Update earlier decisions or claims that a repair changed. See the [output example](references/output-example.md) for structure. End with a continuation note: unresolved issues, and outputs that need rechecking.
